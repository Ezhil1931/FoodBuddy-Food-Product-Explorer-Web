Build a complete, modern, production-quality **Grocery Food Product Catalog Website** using React.

## MAIN GOAL

Create a professional grocery/food product information website where users can:

* Browse all food/grocery products
* Search products
* Filter products by category
* Filter by brand, price, rating, dietary type, and availability
* View product images
* View complete product details
* View ingredients in plain text
* Copy ingredients with one click
* Copy complete product information with one click
* View nutrition information
* View product specifications
* Explore categories
* Read an About/Project page
* Contact the project/company
* Use the website comfortably on mobile, tablet, and desktop

This is **NOT an e-commerce purchasing website yet**.

The project is currently only a **product catalog/information website**.

---

# IMPORTANT — NO BUYING FEATURES

Do NOT implement:

* Cart
* Add to Cart
* Buy Now
* Checkout
* Payment
* Payment gateway
* UPI payment
* Credit/debit card payment
* Cash on Delivery
* Order placement
* Order history
* Shipping calculation
* Delivery payment
* Purchase buttons

Do not create fake purchasing functionality.

The website should focus entirely on **discovering and viewing grocery products and their information**.

The payment/e-commerce functionality may be added in a future version.

